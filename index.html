About

A seasoned veteran of numerous trials, appeals, administrative hearings, arbitrations and mediations, Frank is a member of Dentons Canada’s insurance litigation team. He is recognized as an expert in Lexpert and Best Lawyers, and in 2014 Frank was awarded the Law Society Medal, the Society’s highest honour for outstanding contribution to the profession.

Frank has been a trusted advisor to insurers and other clients for 47 years. He has defended professionals, directors, officers, employees and corporations with respect to claims arising under CGL, E&O, D&O, EPL, product liability, environmental, and other types of coverages.

Frank has extensive experience with complex commercial claims and insurance coverage issues, in particular, dealing with directors and officers (D&O) and errors and omissions (E&O) liability claims. Frank has extensive experience in representing and advising corporations, engineers, surveyors, accountants, financial advisers, insurance brokers and real estate brokers in professional liability claims, corporate and commercial litigation, contract disputes, shareholders disputes, corporate governance matters and product liability claims some of which have involved class actions.

Frank also has a long history of service to the community and the profession, chairing and leading the organization of Canada’s law school mooting competition, the Gale Cup Moot, for over 30 years. He served on his Toronto neighbourhood residents association for over 15 years, including as president. He also served as legal counsel to a major health care not-for-profit, the Multiple Sclerosis Society of Canada, for 34 years handling and coordinating all of their legal requirements.

Experience

  • Juliar v. Attorney General: Lead counsel on this case which established for the first time that courts may rectify errors made by accountants and other professional advisers in tax reorganizations and other matters that mistakenly result in unanticipated tax consequence.
  • Waxman v. Waxman: Successfully defended auditors named in $50+ million shareholder litigation, two year trial and lengthy appeal, affirming auditor duty of care defence established via Hercules case.
  • Polla v. Croatian (Toronto) Credit Union: Lead trial counsel in successful 2018 defence of Board of Directors of Credit Union $5m case brought by investor alleging misrepresentation in Offering Statement.
  • 11867009 Ontario Inc. v. Gerstein: Represented and ultimately reached multi-party settlement of action against directors, officers, and accountants in multi-million dollar real estate litigation alleging negligence and professional liability.
  • Holmes v. Schonfeld: Representing and obtaining dismissal of professional liability litigation against accountants involving complex financial and tax fraud leading to cross-border bankruptcy proceedings.
  • Lipson v. Cassels Brock and Blackwell LLP: Lead counsel defending accounting firm in significant class action arising out of complex tax shelter program.
  • Boards of Directors: Advising several Boards of Directors and individual directors and officers with respect to their D&O coverage in various potential claim scenarios, including bankruptcies of public companies with statutory and other exposures on directors and officers.
  • Coverage opinions: Preparation of numerous coverage opinions for insurers (and to a lesser extent some insureds) relating to various claim scenarios in the context of CGL, environmental, D&O, E&O, EPL and other coverages.
  • Defence Counsel: Appointed by numerous insurers over 42 years to defend professionals, directors, officers, employees, corporations, etc. with respect to claims arising under CGL, E&O, D&O, EPL, product liability, environmental, and other types of coverages.
  • Cyber coverage: Advised insureds on coverage sought for claims advanced by insured’s arising from cyber attacks.
